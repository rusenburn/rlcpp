    How to play

    Placing

    White moves first. On your turn you place a Migo — a plain stone — on any empty square.

    Yugos

    Complete an unbroken line of exactly four of your own pieces and the stone you just placed becomes a Yugo, marked with a dot. Every Migo in those lines is removed; Yugos are permanent and never move or die.

    No long lines

    You may never create an unbroken line of more than four of your own pieces. Squares where that would happen are marked and cannot be played.

    Winning

    Igo: line up four of your own Yugos and you win instantly. Wego: if the player to move has no legal move, the game ends and whoever has more Yugos wins. Equal Yugos is a draw.
